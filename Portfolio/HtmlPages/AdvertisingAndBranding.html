Bread: The Odd Object Using the mundane object of bread for advertising and style studies!

The Odd Object project takes everyday objects and perishable items and utilizes the latest Adobe Ai models to create symbolic, iconic, and indexical imagery. This project’s main intent was to teach the tools of AI image generation in adobe products, exercising the basic skills in creating descriptive prompts in order for the program to produce the best quality image. The images below are highlights from both the results of Adobe Firefly and Express tools and how I utilized descriptive writing. Outside of image generation, this exercise is also practice understanding the principles of design and advertising following the 3 common themes below.

Shape-Image-Meaning Taking an abstract shape and adding textures!

The shape being shown is an illustrator design that was meant to test the AI's capabilities in lighting and depth having lots of curves with a very minimal amount of hard edges and opportunity to become a 3D model. To describe the shape objectively, it was made up of fairly wide rounded edges with no sharp corners, has an asymmetrical build, and has three main protrusions created to create dark and light contrasts on the object. During the Creation process, I didn't go with a specific design in mind but I did go with my memory on an abstract piece I remember seeing in my childhood and basing it off on pure callback knowledge. Regarding the prompt generation, I wanted to add emphasis on shiny, intricate, and small detailed texture objects to showcase the extent of the image generation. As mentioned previously the design of the shape being rounded was to see if the AI can create realistic lighting while turning a flat object into something 3D. To experiment with firefly's AI, I evolved the prompts starting with earthy textures with detail on the type of lighting which then morphed into toning back the lighting prompt details but turning the object into a reflective smooth texture. The images showcased below represent the best of the batch of over 20 images generated for this project. The several images below heavily impressed me with their quality in detail and concept of abstraction but also because they showcase perfectly what I was aiming to experiment with on Firefly. Adding texture and setting to the abstract form allowed the generated images to feel ancient, pricelessness, and in some cases a natural formation. With the metal materials, it made goofy shape into something that can be made into a medal or using a rocky texture added a sense of ancient value.

Onitsuka Branding Using Firefly for brand design!

For this project, we were tasked on utilizing Adobe Firefly and Adobe Photoshop to create 3D logos for brands as if they were being advertised on a poster or bus stop. The brand that I chose for this project was the Japanese clothing brand "Onitsuka Tiger". With the brand in mind, I wanted to visually show the brand as a well refined and established brand.
